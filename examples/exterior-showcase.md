# Summary

This documents the steps to produce an Exterior Showcase video for real estate clients. The final video will include 11 standard clips with an optional set of clips showing special features of a property. The video is set to a soundtrack that is light & upbeat with a rhythm that is well-suited for 8-10 second clips where one clip cuts to the next on the beat.

# Recording Sequence

This defines the sequence in which the drone should record to capture the various clips. The sequence is designed to optimize flight paths for speed of recording so that a minimum amount of time is spent flying the drone from shot to shot. Each shot should capture at least 10-15 seconds of video so that the edit can clip the best 8-10 seconds.

1. **Rise Up** - Position the drone about 50 feet directly above the house so that the house is squarely centered and just a bit of the yard is visible. Begin recording and slowly rise up so that the remainder of the property comes into frame.
2. **Mid Orbit** - Position the drone about 150-200 feet above the side of the house and angle the camera at approximately a 45 degree with the side of the house centered in the frame. Begin recording and orbit the drone **counter-clockwise** completely around the house. This produces both the "Mid Orbit Front and Back" clips.
3. **High Orbit** - Perform the same steps as the **Mid Orbit** except at 300 feet above the house and fly the drone in a **clockwise** orbit. This produces the "High Orbit Front and Back" clips.
4. **High Approach Front** - Position the drone at about 400 feet facing the front of the house with the camera pointed so that the house is centered in the bottom third of the frame. Fly forward and slowly pan the camera down as you approach the house so that it is centered in the frame when the camera is pointed at about a 45-degree angle.
5. **Departure** - Position the drone about 50-100 feet above the house so that the house is centered in the frame. Fly the drone backwards, up and away from the house panning up slowly as you do so that the house is centered in the bottom third of the frame.
6. **Approach Door** - Position the drone about level with the top of the front door frame and near the start of the driveway. Fly the drone forward and towards the door to simulate what a person would see if they were approaching the front door.
7. **Pan Front** - Position the drone at either front corner of the house so that the center of the house is centered in the frame. Fly the drone sideways to the other front corner keeping the house centered. If the front door is not in the center of the house, begin the flight on the corner that is opposite the front door.
8. **Pan Back** - Perform the same maneuver as **Pan Front** except on the back of the house
9. **Features** - Optional - If the house has specific features that the agent wants highlighted, shoot those in a way that is appropriate.

# Clip Edit Sequence

This defines the template to use to edit the clips together into a final video. Each clip should be about 8-10 seconds long depending on the beat of the music. There is a hard cut between clips in time with the beat of the music.

1. **High Approach Front** - This is the intro clip. It should be about 10-14 seconds long to allow time for the fade in and title. It includes a 5 second fade in and includes a title that is the address of the home. The title is visible after the first second of the fade in and is visible for 5 seconds. The music also fades in for 5 seconds.
2. **Mid Orbit Front** - This clip is taken from the **Mid Orbit** recording. It should be about 8-10 seconds and the front of the house should be centered in the frame at the midpoint of the clip (e.g.: the house is centered at 4 seconds for an 8 second clip).
3. **Pan Front** - This gives a closer view of the front of the house.
4. **Approach Door** - This gives the viewer a sense of what it's like to walk up to the front door.
5. **Rise Up** - This gives a top-down look at the house and property.
6. **High Orbit Front** - This clip is taken from the **High Orbit** recording. The front of the house should be centered at the midpoint of the clip.
7. **High Orbit Back** - Same as the **High Orbit Front** clip, except for the back of the house.
8. **Mid Orbit Back** - Same as the **Mid Orbit Front** clip for the back of the house.
9. **Pan Back** - Same as the **Pan Front** clip for the back of the house.
10. **Features** - If there are any special features for the house, those clips are included here.
11. **Departure** - This is the closing clip. It should be 10-20 seconds long. The title overlay fades in quickly at 7 seconds before the end of the clip and remains to the end. A slow fade to black and the music volume also fades starting at 7 seconds before the end.
